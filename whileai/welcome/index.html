Installed

WhileAI is in your toolbar.

Three things, one minute, and the next question you ask gets answered by every AI you use.

01

Pin it

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome and pin WhileAI, so the answers-ready count is always in view.

02

Open the AIs you use, signed in

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek. Your own accounts, your own tabs; there is nothing to configure.

03

Switch on the AIs that should receive your prompts

Open the WhileAI popup and flip the switch next to each AI. Then ask a question in any one of them. The others get it on their own, and the popup tells you when each answer is in.

Every install starts with 14 days of Pro, no card. If something does not work, the questions section covers the usual causes, and mail reaches a person.
