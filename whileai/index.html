How it works

Four steps, and you only do the first one.

01

Sign in to the AIs you use

Nothing to configure. whileAI uses the tabs and accounts you already have, so it never asks for a password or an API key.

02

Pick which ones get your prompts

One switch per AI in the extension panel. An AI you leave off receives nothing.

03

Type your prompt, once

Write it in whichever AI you were going to use anyway. The same text goes to the others, each in its own tab, one at a time.

04

Compare, and keep going

Type the next prompt whenever you like. If an AI is still answering, yours waits in the queue instead of colliding with it.

What the panel tells you

"ready" means that tab is signed in and can receive a prompt. If a site signs you out, the panel says so, so a prompt never disappears quietly.

Your plan shows in the corner, your waiting time so far sits at the top, and the full dashboard is one click away.

Then go and do something else

That is where the name comes from. Start a prompt, switch to your real work, and get a notification when the answer lands.

Where does your AI time actually go?

Every minute in an AI tab is one of three things: writing the prompt, waiting for the answer, or reading it. WhileAI measures all three, from timing alone. It never reads what you type or what the AI writes.

What it will not do

This matters more than any feature, so it is worth being exact.

●
It does not read the answers. It watches whether one is still arriving, so it can time it. The text is never stored or sent anywhere.
●
It does not collect anything about you. No account and no analytics. Your prompts and timings stay in your browser, and you can export or delete them. The few network requests it does make are listed on the privacy page.
●
It does not bypass anything. No CAPTCHA solving, no working around a usage limit, no hidden sessions, no pretending to be a different browser.
●
It does not touch your password. You sign in yourself, in your own browser, as you already do.
●
It stops and tells you. Signed out, rate limited, or a site is showing a verification check: you hear about it rather than finding out an hour later.
