Which AI tools does your team use, and which seats go unused?

A browser extension your admin installs for everyone, once. It records which AI tools are opened and for how long. It never reads a prompt, an answer or a file. You get one report: the tools in use, the seats you pay for that nobody opens, and who is getting value.

Join early access Built with the first five teams. Planned price: $5 per person a month.

Three questions, answered every week

Most teams already pay for AI, often several times over, and nobody can say who uses what.

Which AI tools are in use?

Every AI site opened in your team's browsers: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek and the long tail. How many people use each, and how often. Mark the ones you approve; the rest stand out.

Which seats go unused?

Enter the seats you pay for. See who has not opened their tool this month, and who keeps using a tool you have no seat for, usually on a personal account or paying out of their own pocket.

Who is getting value?

Active people and AI time per team, week by week. Find the people who use it well and the teams that have not started, before you buy more.

Without watching anyone's work

Only timing leaves the browser: which AI site, when, for how long. Never what was typed, what the AI answered, or which files were shared.

How it works

01

Your admin installs it for everyone

Through the Google Admin console or Microsoft Intune, in Chrome and Edge. About ten minutes, no change to anyone's accounts.

02

Everyone is told what is measured

A short notice explains it: which AI sites, when and for how long. Nothing about content.

03

You get the report

A dashboard and a weekly email: tools in use, unused seats, adoption by team.

What it never does, and what it cannot see yet

✕It never reads prompts, answers or files. Only which AI site, when and for how long.
✕It never blocks or slows a tool. It reports; your policy stays yours.
✕No screenshots, no keystroke logging.
–Not covered yet: desktop apps (the ChatGPT and Claude apps), phones, and direct API use. If you need those, say so in the form.
